# Atlas Brand

Atlas is a course-record system for teaching and study. The brand is an instrument: calm, precise, scholarly, quietly premium. It earns trust by being exact, and it sits behind the work rather than narrating itself.

## Idea

An atlas is how you find your position. Course intent is the set of fixed points; alignment is the fix; studying is knowing where you stand. The mark is a quiet course aperture: measured ticks at the top, converging paths, and one precise point of orientation. It renders as white geometry on a very dark hunter green tile.

## Personality

Calm, precise, capable, academic without being institutional or bland.

## At a glance

- Color, type, and tokens live in `app/globals.css` and are described in `docs/DesignSystem.md`.
- Mark: `public/brand/atlas-mark.svg`, rendered throughout the app by `BrandMark` and `AtlasMark`.
- Voice: assume competence. Short labels tied to course work. No anxious or implementation copy.

These notes are a guide, not a constraint. When the artifacts and the prose disagree, the artifacts win.
